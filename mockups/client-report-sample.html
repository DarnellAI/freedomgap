How this projection works

Every figure on this page comes from one projection, run with these assumptions:

  • Investment return: 6.0% a year after fees and fund tax, every year — real markets move around this.
  • Inflation: 2.5% a year. Your target income rises with it; the plan runs to age 90.
  • Age Pension: current rates and thresholds, indexed at 2% a year, applied with the assets and income tests each year.
  • Tax: 2025–26 personal rates; 15% on super contributions and accumulation earnings; pension-phase earnings tax-free.
  • Super stays preserved until age 60, and a full year of income is drawn each retired year.

A projection is a structured "what if", not a promise — small changes in returns move the outcome by years, which is why we review it together every year.
